MY JOURNEY

My Story

Written by Me

PElZX

My name is Fasinasi Oluwapelumi, and I’m known creatively as PelzX. I’m a student and a creative who has always been drawn to the meeting point between technology and art.

I enjoy graphic design, branding, UI/UX, web design and visual storytelling. What started as curiosity has gradually become a personal journey of learning, experimenting, creating and building.

I’m still discovering many parts of my future. I don't expect to know everything at once. Instead, I want to keep learning one skill at a time, practicing through real projects and turning ideas into things people can actually experience.

PelzX represents more than just a name to me. It represents creativity, growth, discipline and the person I am becoming.

My goal is to combine design, technology and problem-solving to create useful digital experiences. I want my work to communicate, inspire and solve real problems.

I am building my future one project, one lesson, one idea and one day at a time.

Professional Roadmap

6 LEVELS

Level 1 — Beginner

Design principles, typography, color, layout and basic tools. Outcome: clean and intentional designs.

Level 2 — Foundation

Advanced graphic design and brand identity. Outcome: coherent visual systems.

Level 3 — Intermediate

Figma, UI, UX research, flows, wireframes and prototypes.

Level 4 — Advanced

Product design, HTML, CSS and responsive websites.

Level 5 — Professional

JavaScript, Git/GitHub, APIs, React, AI-assisted workflows and digital products.

Level 6 — Expert

Specialization, advanced systems thinking, accessibility, performance, leadership, research, mentoring and innovation.

Time-Based Roadmaps

30-Day Roadmap

Week 1: typography, color, hierarchy and composition. Week 2: advanced layout and branding. Week 3: Figma fundamentals and UI. Week 4: one polished UI project and portfolio documentation.

60-Day Roadmap

Days 1–30: design and branding foundation. Days 31–45: UI/UX. Days 46–60: prototype, case study and portfolio improvement.

90-Day Roadmap

Month 1: advanced design + brand identity. Month 2: UI/UX + product thinking. Month 3: HTML/CSS + responsive portfolio site.

6-Month Professional Roadmap

Months 1–2: design/branding. Month 3: UI/UX. Month 4: HTML/CSS. Month 5: JavaScript/Git and front-end foundations. Month 6: portfolio, digital product and capstone.

12-Month Master Roadmap

Months 1–3: design, branding and UI/UX. Months 4–6: web fundamentals and JavaScript. Months 7–9: front-end development, React and digital products. Months 10–12: AI-assisted workflows, professional portfolio, client/job readiness and capstone.
